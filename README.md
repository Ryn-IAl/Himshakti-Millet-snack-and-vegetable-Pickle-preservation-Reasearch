# Himshakti-Millet-snack-and-vegetable-Pickle-preservation-Reasearch
A science-backed research framework for scaling natural preservation techniques for artisanal millet snacks and traditional vegetable pickles.

Project: Natural Hurdles for Heritage Foods
Overview
This research project aims to establish a technical framework for HimShakti to extend the shelf life of its traditional millet snacks and vegetable pickles without the use of synthetic chemical preservatives. By applying Hurdle Technology and Oxidative Barrier Engineering, this project bridges the gap between artisanal production and modern food safety standards.

Problem Statement
Traditional food processing often faces a "Preservation Paradox": maintaining a clean-label, natural status while achieving the 6–12 month shelf stability required for commercial scaling. HimShakti requires evidence-based protocols to manage microbial safety and oxidative rancidity consistently across large batches.

Research Objectives
Microbial Safety: Define intrinsic parameters (pH, water activity) to prevent pathogen growth in pickles.

Oxidative Stability: Identify packaging and processing interventions to prevent rancidity in fat-rich millet snacks.

Regulatory Alignment: Ensure all proposed methods comply with FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) guidelines.

Standardization: Develop actionable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for artisanal production.

Methodology
This project utilizes a systematic literature synthesis approach:

Literature Extraction: Identification of proven shelf-life extension techniques from peer-reviewed food science journals.

Synthesis Matrix: Comparative modeling of preservation methods based on effectiveness, cost, and scalability.

Critical Control Point (CCP) Mapping: Identification of high-risk stages in the production workflow where quality interventions are most effective.

Key Technologies & Tools
Management: Craftybase (Recipe & Batch BOM Management).

Compliance: HACCP-based digital documentation (e.g., Food Ready/Allera).

Documentation: LaTeX and GitHub for version control and research tracking.
